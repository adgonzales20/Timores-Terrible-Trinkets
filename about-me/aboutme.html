Hello, my name is Timore, Supreme Wizard Overlord. I run the shop known as Timore's Terrible Trinkets. My shop is based out in Antarctica and is family run by me and my cousins. Together, we specialize in providing the finest selection of cursed artifacts, forbidden potions, and suspiciously inexpensive magical goods this side of the mortal realm. Ever since I was a young wizard, I have dreamed of conquering the world and bringing all of humanity to its knees. Unfortunately, world domination requires funding, and even an evil wizard has bills to pay. Thus, I established my humble little shop to finance my many ambitious endeavors. Every purchase brings me one step closer to achieving my ultimate goal, so please, shop generously! Your contributions are greatly appreciated, even if you are entirely unaware of what you are supporting. In my spare time, I enjoy experimenting with ancient curses, trapping the souls of those who inconvenience me, and finding creative new ways to exploit the unfortunate. I also enjoy long walks through frozen wastelands and listening to the screams of my enemies, which I find particularly soothing during the colder months. My philosophy is simple: why settle for ordinary merchandise when you can purchase something that may permanently alter your existence? So come on in, browse our selection, and remember: at Timore's Terrible Trinkets, your misery is our business!
